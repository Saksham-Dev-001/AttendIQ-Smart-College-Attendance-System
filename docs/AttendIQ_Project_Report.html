EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Abstract & Key Metrics

Traditional classroom attendance in Indian higher education institutions relies heavily on paper register sheets or static paper roll-calls, consuming 10–15 minutes of every 50-minute instructional period (~25% of active learning time). Even rudimentary digital systems that display static QR codes fail catastrophically due to "proxy attendance" enabled by instant screenshot sharing over messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram).

AttendIQ is a production-hardened, multi-tier Progressive Web App (PWA) engineered specifically for Sri Balaji College of Engineering & Technology (SBCET), Jaipur. AttendIQ eliminates proxy attendance through a synchronized four-stage verification pipeline: (1) Short-lived dynamic QR tokens rotating every 25 seconds with cryptographic entropy; (2) GPS Haversine geofencing enforcing physical student presence within a configurable campus radius (50m–500m) with fallback Campus Wi-Fi Beacon verification; (3) In-browser camera facial recognition matched against enrollment records with randomized interactive liveness challenges (blink, smile, head-turn) to defeat photographic presentation attacks; and (4) Atomic composite key uniqueness locks prohibiting duplicate check-ins.

CHAPTER 1

Project Overview & Objectives

1.1 Institutional Background

Sri Balaji College of Engineering & Technology (SBCET), Jaipur, established in 2000, conducts undergraduate engineering degree programs affiliated with Rajasthan Technical University (RTU), Kota. RTU statutory guidelines mandate that students maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each theory and laboratory subject to be eligible to sit for end-semester university examinations.

1.2 Existing Bottlenecks & Vulnerabilities

  • Instructional Time Depletion: Faculty spend between 10 to 15 minutes of every 50-minute lecture verbally calling roll numbers, reducing semester curriculum coverage by up to 25%.
  • Buddy Punching & Proxy Signatures: In large lecture halls (65+ students in Section A), students routinely sign paper sheets on behalf of absent peers or whisper roll calls.
  • Digital Proxy via Screenshot Sharing: First-generation QR systems display static QR codes. Absent students sitting in hostels or off-campus receive instant photos via WhatsApp and mark attendance remotely.
  • Administrative Delays & Manual Register Compilation: Calculating monthly attendance percentages across 6 branches and 114 students requires days of manual clerical calculation.

CHAPTER 3

Multi-Layer Anti-Fraud Pipeline

3.1 Layer 1: Dynamic Short-Lived QR Tokens

To thwart screenshot-based proxy attendance, AttendIQ generates rotating time-bound tokens rather than static URLs. On the faculty display, the QR token rotates automatically every 25 seconds.

  • Token Structure: ATTENDIQ:{sessionId}:{timestamp}:{salt} encoded with cryptographic entropy.
  • Quick-Code Support: Generates a companion 6-character alphanumeric quick-code for high-entropy manual entry in case of camera hardware limitations.
  • Grace-Period Tolerance: The verification service implements a 30-second clock-drift window, permitting students who scanned 2 seconds before rotation to validate cleanly while rejecting screenshots older than one cycle.

3.2 Layer 2: Haversine Campus Geofencing

The system queries device GPS coordinates via navigator.geolocation.getCurrentPosition using high-accuracy mode. Distance between the student's current position and the lecture hall is computed using the spherical Haversine Formula: 

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ₁) ⋅ cos(φ₂) ⋅ sin²(Δλ/2)
c = 2 ⋅ atan2( √a, √(1−a) )
d = R ⋅ c   (where R = 6,371,000 meters)

The default campus geofence is anchored at 26.9855° N, 75.7725° E (SBCET Campus, Benad Road, Jaipur) with an institutional radius of 150 meters.

Location Permission Denial Recovery Protocol
If a student denies browser location access or is inside a shielded basement lab where GPS signals attenuate, AttendIQ activates the Campus Wi-Fi Beacon Fallback. The student connects to the authorized institutional SSID (SBCET-STUDENT-5G) and validates through the college subnet gateway.

3.3 Layer 3: Biometric Face Match & Liveness Detection

To guarantee that the student holding the device matches the enrolled roll number, AttendIQ accesses the front-facing webcam stream inside a facial framing viewport:

  • Facial Verification: Live canvas frame capture compares student facial landmarks against enrollment biometric profiles stored during admission.
  • Interactive Anti-Spoofing Liveness Challenge: The user is prompted with randomized interactive challenges: "Please blink both eyes naturally now", "Smile at the camera", or "Turn your head slightly to the left". Temporal frame analysis verifies motion, defeating static photo or pre-recorded video presentation attacks.

3.4 Layer 4: Atomic Duplicate Prevention Lock

To eliminate double submissions, the database enforces an atomic composite key: rec_{sessionId}_{studentId}. Once written, subsequent attempts within the same session throw a DUPLICATE_ATTENDANCE_LOCK exception.

4.2 Service Worker Caching Architecture (public/sw.js)

The service worker employs a dual-strategy caching pattern:

  • Navigation Requests (HTML shell): Network-First strategy falling back to the precached /index.html shell when college Wi-Fi experiences network dropouts.
  • Static Bundles (JS, CSS, Icons, Fonts): Stale-While-Revalidate strategy ensuring instant UI load times while background-updating assets on subsequent visits.

4.3 Apple Mobile & Home-Screen Integration

Configured in index.html with apple-mobile-web-app-capable, apple-mobile-web-app-status-bar-style="black-translucent", and iOS touch icons, enabling seamless 1-tap installation via Safari's "Add to Home Screen".

CHAPTER 6

Teacher Command Center & Day-Wise Timetable

The Faculty portal in AttendIQ is organized into three major workflows: Day-Wise Lecture Scheduling, Live Attendance Broadcasting, and Historical Course Registers.

6.1 Day-Wise Teaching Schedule Navigator

Rather than displaying an unorganized list of classes, TeacherDashboard.tsx features an intuitive day-selector tab interface (Monday through Saturday, plus an "All Days" view). When selected:

  • Classes for that specific day are loaded in chronological order by start time.
  • Displays Subject Code, Full Name, Assigned Section, Classroom, and Period Duration.
  • Indicates real-time status: Scheduled, Live Session Active, or Completed.
  • One-tap button to "Start Attendance" or "Resume Live Screen".

6.2 Live Attendance Session (TeacherLiveSession.tsx)

  • Dynamic QR Display: Center-stage high-contrast QR code rotating every 25 seconds with an animated countdown ring.
  • Live Attendance Counter: Real-time display showing present count vs. total section enrollment (e.g. 62 / 65 Present — 95.4%).
  • Real-Time Stream Feed: Instant animated ticker showing students checking in with verification metadata (QR Valid, GPS Distance, Face Match Score).
  • Instant PDF Export Button: Direct header action to print or save the session roll call sheet as PDF right from class.

Automated PDF Reporting Engine

CHAPTER 8

Student Experience & Scan Wizard

The student interface is mobile-first, lightweight, and structured to guide the student through the four verification checks in under 10 seconds:

8.1 Multi-Step Verification Flow (StudentScanWizard.tsx)

  1. Step 1: Session Discovery & QR Scan: Automatically identifies active faculty broadcasts or allows camera scanning of the dynamic QR.
  2. Step 2: Campus Geofence Validation: Reads GPS coordinates and computes distance to the classroom anchor. Displays distance in meters (e.g. "18m from SL1 Lecture Hall — Verified").
  3. Step 3: Biometric Face & Liveness Match: Activates front camera with animated framing oval. Analyzes facial features and challenges the student to blink or smile.
  4. Step 4: Digital Attendance Receipt: Triggers confetti celebration with digital verification receipt, cryptographic transaction hash, and updated subject attendance percentage.

8.2 Analytics & Shortage Warning Gauge

The student dashboard features a circular SVG attendance gauge. If the student's overall or subject-wise attendance drops below 75%, an automated amber/red shortage alert is rendered, detailing exactly how many consecutive classes must be attended to regain university eligibility.
